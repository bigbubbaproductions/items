Brass Chest drop rates

BUBBA PEG SURVIVORS gives a Brass Chest for every 20 minutes of active play, a one-time Welcome Chest, and a chest the first time a player reaches each of about a dozen milestone achievements. Chests are earned through play. The game does not sell chests or keys. Opening a chest gives one cosmetic, picked when it opens, at these rates.

Every finish and solid colour item also comes in 5 special looks, each an item of its own: Dewbow, Gilded, Aurora, Kilnglow and Wellshine. Of every 1,000 drops of an item and its looks, 995 are the item and 1 is each look. Plain items have no special looks. Every copy of an item is the same.

A chest uses the rates of the difficulty most of its 20 minutes were played on. The Welcome Chest and the achievement chests use the Gentle and Steady rates.
